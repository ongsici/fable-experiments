## Design

**Strategy.** The submarine parks at column 8 facing left (back cell at column 9) and only moves vertically after that. Fire is the default action, so its bullets sweep the whole row to its left.

- **Positioning (row 0, which is safe):** move right until the sub touches column 9, then one step left to end at column 8 facing left, then dive.
- **Hunting (rows 2–8):** move toward rows that hold an enemy in columns 0–4, nearest first. Stay and fire when one is in the current row. Idle at row 5.
- **Dodging:** leave the row if anything dangerous is at columns 5–7 (an incoming bullet) or column 9 (an enemy spawning behind).
- **Never enter** a row with danger at columns 5–9, and never move toward a row two away with danger at columns 7–9, because a sticky action can repeat the move.
- **Divers:** a diver at columns 7–9 within three rows pulls the sub to that row, where it waits for the diver to step onto column 8.
- **Oxygen:** when oxygen is below 60 the sub rises. Rows 2→1→0 are only crossed if the diver bar shows at least one diver; after refilling it dives again.

**Hidden layer 1** (19 units used). Each unit is gated by the sub's row: the two player cells add +20000 against a −20000 bias, so only the unit for the current row is unsaturated and all others sit at exactly −1.

| Units | Role |
|---|---|
| 0, 1, 2 | Row 0: move right, move left, dive |
| 3 | Row 1: keep descending |
| 4, 5 | Rows 1 and 2: go up only when surfacing (has diver and low oxygen) |
| 6–11 | "Go up" score for rows 3–8 |
| 12–17 | "Go down" score for rows 2–7 |
| 18 | Row 2: weak emergency escape upward |

The up/down scores add these terms, from weakest to strongest: target pull ±(2…7), hold on a target in my row −30, low oxygen ±100, diver ±250, threat in my row +1000, entry veto −20000.

**Hidden layer 2.** Four units sum the up, down, right and left families and re-squash them. The outputs are 12 × those, with fire at +2 and no-op at −20.
