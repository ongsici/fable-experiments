## Design

**Strategy.** The submarine parks at column 8 facing left and never moves sideways underwater, so everything reduces to row logic.

- **Homing (row 0 only):** move right to column 9, turn left once to reach column 8 facing left, then dive. Row 0 is safe and costs no oxygen.
- **Row scoring:** each row gets a linear score from its danger, reward and trail cells. The weights make a row:
  - *good* for an enemy approaching from the left in columns 0–5, an enemy that has passed and is leaving in columns 0–4, or a diver about to reach column 8;
  - *bad* for any enemy bullet in columns 5–9, or any enemy in columns 6–9 (including one just spawned on the right);
  - neutral otherwise. Trail weights cancel divers that have already passed.
- **Behaviour underwater:**
  - in a good row: stay and fire;
  - in a bad row: dodge up, or down if the row above is bad;
  - otherwise: move towards a good row, preferring up, and never step into a bad row;
  - with nothing to do: no-op, which keeps the shot ready.
- **Row 1 is transit only.** A sticky repeat of "up" from row 1 would surface with no diver and end the episode.
- **Surfacing:** when oxygen is below 60 and at least one diver is on board, go up; below 20, go up even through bad rows.

**Hidden layer 1 (27 units used):**
- units 0–7: thermometer of the player row (row ≤ k);
- units 8–14: "row r is good" for rows 2–8;
- units 15–22: "row r is bad" for rows 1–8;
- unit 23: surface now (diver on board and oxygen < 60);
- unit 24: home (back cell at row 0, column 9);
- unit 25: turn (at column 9 facing right on row 0);
- unit 26: oxygen critical (diver on board and oxygen < 20).

**Hidden layer 2 (25 units used),** each gated on the player's row:
- units 0–7: "go up" for rows 2–8 and row 1;
- units 8–14: "go down" for rows 1–7;
- units 15–17: go right, go left, dive (row 0);
- units 18–24: "fire" for rows 2–8.

Output logits are 60 for up, 40 for down, fire, left or right, and 20 for no-op, so sampling is effectively deterministic.
