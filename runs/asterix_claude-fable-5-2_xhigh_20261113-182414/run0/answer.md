## Design

**Strategy.** The player never moves left or right, so it stays in column 5 for the whole episode and only chooses no-op, up or down. Because the column is fixed, each row's threat or opportunity is a fixed function of that row's cells. The weights are hand-derived and untested, since nothing could be run.

Each action gets a utility, and the large logit scale makes the softmax close to an argmax:

- **Danger.** Each action is penalised by how soon the enemy in the row it leads to reaches column 5. An approaching enemy at distance ≤1 or on column 5 costs about 1000, distance 2 costs 300, distance 3 costs 60, distance 4 costs 10, distance 5 costs 4. A receding enemy costs 0. The player therefore leaves its row early, which gives several attempts against sticky no-ops.
- **Sticky-move lookahead.** Up and down carry an extra cost (25 alone, much more combined with other danger) if the row two away has an enemy about to reach column 5, since a repeated move would carry the player into it.
- **Treasure.** An approaching treasure at distance d from column 5 has reach d+1 rows. Each reachable treasure above adds +18 to up, each below adds +12 to down, and one approaching in the player's own row adds +40 to no-op.
- **Housekeeping.** No-op has a +6 bias; rows 1–2 and 7–8 get a mild push back towards the centre; up at row 1 and down at row 8 are blocked; left, right and fire sit at −5000.

**Layer 1 (31 units used).** Direction comes from the trail: each row's cost is an exact linear function of its enemy and trail cells, solved so that approaching gives the cost and receding gives 0.

- Units 0–7, `Stay_r`: gated by "player in row r", output the danger of row r.
- Units 8–14, `Up_r` (r=2..8): gated the same way, danger of row r−1 plus lookahead on row r−2.
- Units 15–21, `Down_r` (r=1..7): danger of row r+1 plus lookahead on row r+2.
- Units 22–29, `G_r`: treasure reach of row r, encoded as −1 + 0.25·reach (−1 if none or receding).
- Unit 30: player row as a near-linear analogue value.

**Layer 2 (30 units used).**

- Units 0–2: linear pass-through of the summed stay, up and down costs.
- Unit 3: player row pass-through.
- Units 4–5: "at row 1" and "at row 8".
- Units 6–12, `A_r`: true when player row − r ≤ reach of row r (treasure above within reach).
- Units 13–19, `B_r`: the same for treasure below.
- Units 20–27: detect approaching treasure in the player's own row.
- Units 28–29: "row ≤ 2" and "row ≥ 7".
